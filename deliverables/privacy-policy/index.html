Privacy Policy — Image to PDF

Developer: Paul Digital · Last updated: 2026

Image to PDF ("the app") lets you convert images into PDF documents. We designed the app to be private by default. This policy explains what data is and is not processed.

1. Local Image Processing

All image selection, cropping, rotation, compression and PDF generation happen entirely on your device. Your images and the PDFs you create are never uploaded to our servers, to Cloudflare, to GitHub, or to any third party.

2. Local File Storage

Generated PDFs are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. A list of your recent PDFs (file name, size, page count and creation date) is stored locally so you can reopen them. You can delete any PDF at any time from within the app.

3. Data We Collect

The app itself does not collect personal information and does not require an account or login. We do not include any analytics SDKs.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google's AdMob SDK may collect and process certain data such as device identifiers, approximate location (derived from IP), and ad interaction data. This is handled by Google, not by us. Learn more in Google's Advertising policy and Google Privacy Policy. You can manage ad personalization in your device settings.

5. Permissions

  • Photos / Media: Used only when you choose images to convert. We use the Android system photo picker where possible, which does not require broad storage access.
  • Camera: Requested only if you choose "Take Photo", so you can capture a page to add to your PDF.

Permissions are requested only when needed, and denying an optional permission does not break the app.

6. Remote Configuration

The app fetches a small, non-personal configuration file (for example, whether ads are enabled and which ad unit IDs to use) from our Cloudflare service. This request contains no personal data and is cached on your device. Core Image-to-PDF functionality works fully offline even if this service is unavailable.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your images and PDFs never leave your device, you are always in control. Deleting a PDF in the app removes it from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

10. Contact
